Playa Gigante with kids

Tide pools, calm coves, a bunk room for the crew, and a village pace — why Gigante is one of the easiest family beach trips in Central America.

Playa Gigante works for families in a way that bigger resorts don't. It's small, safe-feeling, and outdoors all day — tide pools before breakfast, a private pool at noon, a first surf lesson in the afternoon. And because we handle the transfer, the groceries, and a chef if you want one, parents actually get a holiday too. Here's how to plan it.

Why it's great for kids

Gigante is a working fishing village, not a party town — the rhythm is slow and the days revolve around the water. There's a natural variety of things to do within steps of the house, the coves are calm, and there's always a grown-up nearby who knows the beach. It's the rare trip where a six-year-old and a sixteen-year-old are both happy.

Things to do with kids

  • Tide pools & the sea shelf: Shankton Harbour has its own rock shelf about twenty steps from the house — endless tide-pooling, snorkeling, and reef fishing at high tide. Kids lose hours here.
  • Private pool: both homes have a pool, so there's always safe, easy swimming right at the house.
  • First surf lesson: the nearby beach breaks are forgiving, and our team lines up gentle lessons for beginners. See the surf guide.
  • Easy fishing trip: a calm inshore panga trip is a brilliant half-day with kids — captains rig everything. See the fishing guide.
  • Walk to the village: a short, shaded jungle trail leads to the beach restaurants for fresh seafood and a look at the pangas coming in.

Which home for a family?

Shankton Tower is the easy call for bigger groups — it sleeps 12 and has a dedicated bunk room for the kids, four floors of space, and a cliff-top pool. Shankton Harbour is perfect for smaller families: its private sea shelf is a natural playground, and it sleeps 8. Both sit in a gated community with an on-site caretaker. Not sure? Our which-home guide compares them side by side.

Getting there with children

This is the part parents worry about, and it's genuinely easy. We arrange a private door-to-door transfer with our driver Danny, who meets you at Managua airport, helps with bags and car seats, and stops at the Colonia supermarket in Rivas on the way so you arrive with everything the kids need. One car, one price, no shuttles to chase after a long flight. Full details in our getting-there guide.

What makes it easy on parents

  • Private chef on request — no restaurant battles; eat well at the house on the kids' schedule.
  • Grocery stop included so the fridge is stocked on arrival.
  • On-site caretaker and a team (Nemer) who arranges everything from lessons to boat trips.
  • Filtered drinking water is provided at both homes.
  • A pool at the house means downtime is always an option.

A few parent notes

  • Water safety: the coves are calm but it's the open Pacific — keep little ones close in the surf and watch footing on the tide-pool rocks.
  • Sun: it's strong year-round; pack reef-safe sunscreen, hats, and rash guards.
  • When to come: see the best-time-to-visit guide — green season is lush and quiet, dry season is reliably sunny.
  • Bring: any specific kids' medicine, and check current travel guidance before you travel.
