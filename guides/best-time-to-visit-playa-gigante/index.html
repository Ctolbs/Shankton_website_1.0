Best time to visit Playa Gigante

Dry season or green season, surf or sunshine — an honest month-by-month on when to come to Nicaragua's emerald coast.

Playa Gigante is warm and swimmable all year — there's no bad time to come, only different flavors of good. The choice really comes down to what you're after: guaranteed sun, the biggest surf, the lushest scenery, or the lowest rates. Here's how the year breaks down, from people who run houses on this beach.

The short answer

If you want one recommendation: aim for late October through early December. The green season's jungle is still lush, the sun is coming back, the surf is firing, the crowds are thin, and rates are lower than the December–April peak. That said — the coast delivers year-round, so read on for the season that fits your trip.

The two seasons

Nicaragua's Pacific coast runs on two seasons, and knowing which you're booking into tells you almost everything.

SeasonMonthsWhat to expect
Dry season
(verano)		 Mid-Nov – April Reliable sunshine, minimal rain, clean offshore breezes. Busiest and priciest, especially over the holidays. By late dry season (Feb–April) the hills turn golden-brown and dusty.
Green season
(invierno)		 May – Mid-Nov Short, warm afternoon or evening downpours — not all-day storms. Mornings are usually clear. The landscape is at its greenest, the ocean's warm, crowds are low, and rates are the best of the year.

One myth worth busting: green season is not a washout. Rain typically arrives as a brief, dramatic tropical downpour that clears as fast as it came, most often in the late afternoon. You still get sunny mornings for the beach and surf — you just pack a light rain layer.

Best time for surf

The biggest, most consistent swells arrive with the southern-hemisphere winter, roughly April to October. What makes this coast special, though, is wind: thanks to the thermal pull off Lake Nicaragua, offshore winds blow here on the majority of days — strongest in the dry season — grooming the waves clean. Put those two together and the local sweet spot is the October–November shoulder: real swell, returning offshore mornings, and green-season prices. Popoyo works year-round. For breaks, seasons, and skill levels, see our Playa Gigante surf guide.

Best time for sunshine and dry weather

Want to bank on blue skies? The heart of the dry season, December through April, is your window — day after day of sun, warm nights, and calm, clear beach days. It's also the most popular stretch, so book early, especially around Christmas, New Year, and Semana Santa (Easter week), which is the single busiest week on the coast.

Best time for value and quiet

The green season (May–October) is the insider's pick. You trade a bit of afternoon rain for lush scenery, warm water, empty line-ups, and the lowest nightly rates of the year. September and October are the quietest and greenest of all — and if you don't mind a passing shower, the surf is often excellent. It's when the coast feels most like a secret.

Sea turtles and wildlife

Two protected refuges near Gigante — La Flor and Chacocente — host arribadas, the mass nesting of olive ridley sea turtles, roughly July through December. Watching thousands of turtles come ashore under the stars is one of the most memorable things you can do here. Our on-site GM, Nemer, can arrange a guided visit during nesting season — just ask.

How hot does it get?

Warm, always. Daytime highs sit around 30–33°C (86–91°F) most of the year, cooling pleasantly at night in the dry season. The ocean stays near 27–29°C — pure boardshorts water, no wetsuit needed. The hottest, stickiest stretch is April and May, right before the rains break the heat.

So — when should you go?

  • First trip / want it easy and sunny: December–April (dry season). Book holidays and Semana Santa well ahead.
  • Best all-rounder: late October to early December — sun returning, surf pumping, green hills, lower rates.
  • Surfers chasing size: April–September, with offshore-clean shoulders around the edges.
  • Budget and solitude: the green season, especially September–October.
  • Sea turtles: July–December, peaking in the autumn months.

A few practical notes

  • Getting here doesn't change with the season, but the final dirt road into Gigante is easier in the dry months; in green season a higher-clearance vehicle helps. See getting to Playa Gigante.
  • Pack for sun year-round — reef-safe sunscreen, a hat, and a light rain layer May–November.
  • Book early for the dry-season peak. Holidays and Semana Santa fill months in advance; green-season dates are far more flexible.

Common questions

What is the best month to visit Playa Gigante?

For most travelers, late October through early December is the sweet spot: the green season's lush landscape lingers, the sun is returning, the surf is still firing, and rates are lower than the December–April peak. If you want guaranteed sunshine, come in the dry season (mid-November to April).

When is the rainy (green) season in Nicaragua?

The green season on Nicaragua's Pacific coast runs roughly May to mid-November. Rain usually comes as short, warm afternoon or evening downpours rather than all-day storms, and mornings are often clear. The landscape is at its greenest and visitor numbers are low.

Is Playa Gigante worth visiting in the rainy season?

Yes. Green-season rain is typically brief and tropical, mornings are often sunny, the surf is consistent, and you get lush scenery, fewer crowds, and lower rates. Pack a light rain layer and plan beach time for the mornings.

When is the surf best at Playa Gigante?

The biggest, most consistent swells arrive with the southern-hemisphere winter, roughly April to October. Nicaragua's famous offshore winds are strongest in the dry season, so the shoulder months around October and November — swell plus clean offshore mornings — are the local sweet spot. Popoyo works year-round.

Can you see sea turtles near Playa Gigante?

Yes — the La Flor and Chacocente wildlife refuges nearby host olive ridley turtle arribadas roughly July through December. Our team can arrange a guided visit during nesting season.

How hot does it get at Playa Gigante?

It's warm year-round, with daytime highs around 30–33°C (86–91°F) and warm ocean water near 27–29°C — boardshorts weather, no wetsuit needed. The hottest, driest stretch is April and May, just before the rains return.
