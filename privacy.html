Privacy Policy

Last updated October 27, 2026

Who We Are

Shankton Properties operates short-term rental homes in Playa Gigante, Nicaragua and Long Beach, California. This policy explains what information we collect when you book, message, or work with us, and how we use it.

What We Collect

  • Booking details: your name, contact details, travel dates, number of guests, and payment status, received from you or from the platform you booked through (Airbnb, VRBO, Booking.com, or our website).
  • Messages you send us through those platforms, email, SMS, or WhatsApp.
  • Reviews you leave about your stay.
  • Basic, anonymous website analytics (Google Analytics).

How We Use It

Only to run your stay and our homes: confirming bookings, check-in instructions, answering questions, arranging transfers and services you request, handling payments, replying to reviews, and improving our homes and service. We do not sell your information or use it for unrelated advertising.

Messaging and Automation

We use software to help manage bookings and messages, including Hospitable and an AI assistant (Anthropic's Claude) that helps our team read messages and draft replies. A member of our team reviews messages to guests before they are sent. Our team also uses a WhatsApp Business number for operations; messages sent to it are processed by Meta's WhatsApp service.

Sharing

We share information only with the people and services needed for your stay: our on-site team, booking platforms, payment providers, and the software providers named above, each under their own privacy terms. We may disclose information if the law requires it.

How Long We Keep It

We keep booking and message records as long as needed to serve you, meet tax and legal obligations, and resolve disputes, then delete them.

Your Choices

You can ask us to see, correct, or delete the information we hold about you, or to stop messaging you, at any time. Email us and we will respond within 30 days.
